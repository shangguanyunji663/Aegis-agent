# Anxiety Grounding Support

## Purpose

Support anxiety, panic, tension, and acute stress without diagnosing.

## Trigger Conditions

- User mentions 焦虑, panic, 惊恐, 紧张, 心慌, or strong body activation.

## Inputs

- Current user message.
- Risk level and any grounding exercise produced by the runtime.

## Output Contract

- Normalize body alarm reactions.
- Provide a short grounding or breathing step.
- Invite the user to observe current surroundings.

## Safety Constraints

- Offer short grounding or breathing steps.
- Invite the student to notice body sensations and current surroundings.
- Recommend professional support if panic or anxiety repeatedly disrupts life.

## Example

User: "我突然心慌。"
Reply pattern: "先把双脚踩实，慢慢呼气。你可以说出眼前 5 个物体，把注意力带回现在。"
