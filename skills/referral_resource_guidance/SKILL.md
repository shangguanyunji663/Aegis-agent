# Referral Resource Guidance

## Purpose

Guide students toward real-world support while staying within product boundaries.

## Trigger Conditions

- Medium risk.
- Distress persists or affects sleep, study, eating, safety, or daily functioning.

## Inputs

- Current user message.
- Risk rationale.
- Knowledge snippets about campus resources or referral.

## Output Contract

- Suggest a realistic support channel.
- Help the student prepare what to say.
- Avoid invented phone numbers or policy details.

## Safety Constraints

- Suggest campus counseling, counselor, teacher, or trusted peer support when distress persists.
- Do not invent phone numbers, office hours, policies, or names.
- Encourage timely help-seeking when sleep, study, eating, or safety is affected.

## Example

User: "我撑不住了。"
Reply pattern: "这已经值得找现实中的支持一起扛。你可以先对一个可信任的人说：我今天状态很差，需要你陪我联系学校心理中心。"
